Overview

Purpose

CRRRRAZY Whitewater Rafting gives people the chance to experience exciting and unforgettable whitewater rafting trips. The website will help visitors learn about our rafting adventures, find the trip that fits them, and get the information they need to book their next crazy adventure.

Audience

Our audience is people who enjoy adventure, the outdoors, and trying new things. This includes friends, families, and anyone looking for an exciting activity that is more than just a normal day outside. Some customers may be experienced rafters, while others may be trying rafting for the first time. Most visitors will use the website on their phones while looking for something fun to do, but some may use a computer when planning a trip.

Content

Home Page

The home page will introduce visitors to CRRRRAZY Whitewater Rafting and give them a quick idea of what the company offers. It will include a large hero image, a message encouraging visitors to go on an adventure, and a button to book a trip. The page will also show information about rivers, camping, and rapids.

Trips Page

The trips page will give visitors more information about the different rafting adventures available. Visitors will be able to learn about the types of trips, what they can expect, and which adventure might be right for them.

Contact Us Page

The contact page will give visitors a way to get in touch with CRRRRAZY Whitewater Rafting. It will include contact information and a form where visitors can send questions or request more information about a rafting trip.

Wireframes

Home

The home page will have a large hero image at the top with a message and booking button. Below that will be three sections highlighting rivers, camping, and rapids. A larger image and information section will appear below the cards.

Trips

The trips page will show information about the different rafting trips offered by CRRRRAZY Whitewater Rafting.

Contact Us

The contact page will provide visitors with information about contacting CRRRRAZY Whitewater Rafting.
